Our Story

A Labor of Love,
Churned Daily

Born in 2009 from a small kitchen and a big dream, Srav's Ice Cream Parlour has grown into a beloved neighborhood destination. We believe the best ice cream comes from the best ingredients — sourced fresh, crafted with care, and served with a smile.

Every scoop is a story. Whether you're celebrating a milestone or just cooling off on a sunny afternoon, we're here to make that moment sweeter.

100% Fresh

Locally sourced dairy, fruits, and nuts with no artificial preservatives.

Premium Quality

Stringent quality checks at every step of our artisanal process.

Family First

A welcoming space designed for every age, with something for everyone.
